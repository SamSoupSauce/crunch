CRUNCH — Official Rules

Universal Control Protocol & Movement Mechanics

1. Overview & Objective

Crunch is a two-player, turn-based abstract strategy game played on a hexagonal grid. The core concept revolves around maneuvering and weaponizing neutral obstacles to trap the enemy between "A Rock and a Hard Place".

Objective: Win the game by forcing the opponent's mover into a lethal trap—pushing a rock into them when they cannot retreat—thereby executing a Crunch, or by forcing the opponent to surrender.

2. Components & Board Setup

The Board

  • Hexagonal Matrix: Standard board radius $R = 5$ contains 61 hexagonal spaces.
  • Six Vectors: Movement occurs along 6 cardinal axes ($60^\circ$ increments).
  • Outer Edge: Outer boundary forms a solid wall. Nothing can leave the board.

Pieces

  • Movers: Player 1 (Blue) and Player 2 (Red).
  • Rocks: 16 neutral rocks (8 on each side in standard setup).
  • Universal Ownership: Rock colors are cosmetic/positional. Once play begins, either player can push any rock on the board.

3. Turn Structure

  1. First Move: Blue (Player 1) always takes the first turn.
  2. Turn Flow: Players alternate turns. Exactly one action per turn.
  3. No Passing: A player must make a legal move on their turn.
  4. Game End: Play continues until a mover is crushed (Crunch) or a player surrenders.

4. Movement Mechanics

A player controls only their respective mover. Each turn, perform one of three actions:

A. Simple Move:

Move one space into any adjacent empty hex.

B. Pushing a Rock:
  • Move into the rock's hex, displacing it one space forward in that same direction (follow-through).
  • Single-Piece Force Limit: Only push one rock at a time. Cannot push two rocks in a row.
  • Valid Destination: Destination hex must be empty. Cannot push rocks off the board or into another rock.
  • Universal Manipulation: Any rock can be pushed by either mover.
C. Pushing the Opposing Mover:

A mover can push the opposing mover if the space directly behind them is an empty hex. Both movers advance one space.

5. Hazardous Conditions & Winning (The "Crunch")

A player achieves Crunch and immediately wins the match when pushing a rock into the opposing mover under Hazardous Conditions.

Hazardous Conditions occur when:
  1. Mover A pushes a rock into Mover B's current hex, AND
  2. The space directly behind Mover B is blocked by either a wall (outer board boundary) or another rock.

When both conditions are met, Mover B is crushed between the incoming rock and a hard place — immediate victory for Mover A!

6. End of Game

  • Crunch Achieved: A player crushes the opponent under Hazardous Conditions.
  • Surrender: A player chooses to resign at any point during their turn.
