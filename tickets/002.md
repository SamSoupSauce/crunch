## 2. DETAILED SPECIFICATIONS FOR THE 5 CURSED MODIFIERS

### MODIFIER 1: "Newton's Cradle" (Kinetic Shockwave)
- **Concept:** Abolish the single-piece force limit by treating continuous collinear rock chains as rigid elastic bodies.
- **Trigger:** Mover pushes into an adjacent rock that has 1 or more rocks directly behind it along the push vector `V`.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. Let the chain of consecutive contiguous rocks along vector `V` be `R_1, R_2, ..., R_k` (where `k >= 2`).
  2. The pusher moves into the cell previously occupied by `R_1`.
  3. Intermediate rocks `R_1` through `R_{k-1}` remain stationary in their current hexes.
  4. The terminus rock `R_k` absorbs all kinetic energy and is displaced 1 hex forward to `Position(R_k) + V`.
  5. **Hazardous Crunch Check:** If `Position(R_k) + V` is occupied by an opposing mover whose retreat is blocked (wall or rock), the terminus rock executes an immediate long-range CRUNCH win.
  6. If `Position(R_k) + V` is an outer wall or petrified wall tile, the chain is locked and cannot be pushed.

### MODIFIER 2: "Tectonic Shrink" (Sudden Death Ring Collapse)
- **Concept:** Battle royale style perimeter decay to eliminate turtling and force confrontations.
- **Trigger:** A turn counter tracks consecutive turns without a Crunch.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. Every 8 completed turns (4 rounds per player) without a decisive crunch, the current outer radius collapses: `CurrentMaxRadius = CurrentMaxRadius - 1` (floored at `R = 2`).
  2. **Warning Phase:** At turn 7, 15, 23, etc., flash the collapsing ring on the SVG board with a pulsing red fissure warning.
  3. **Collapse Phase:** At turn 8, 16, 24, etc., all hexes with axial distance equal to `CurrentMaxRadius + 1` permanently turn into void cells (`TileVoid`).
  4. **Crushed / Fallen Entities:** Any neutral rock on a void cell is deleted from the active pool. Any mover caught on a collapsing tile immediately perishes, awarding the surviving player a Ring Out victory.

### MODIFIER 3: "Frictionless Granite" (Air Hockey / Ice Board)
- **Concept:** Negate ground friction. Rocks behave like frictionless sliding pucks.
- **Trigger:** A mover pushes a rock along vector `V`.
- **Mechanics:**
  1. The pusher takes 1 step forward into the rock's starting hex (normal follow-through).
  2. Instead of moving only 1 hex, the pushed rock continues traveling along vector `V` step-by-step until:
     - It encounters another rock or mover.
     - It hits an outer wall or petrified wall tile.
     - It reaches the boundary of the board.
  3. **Lethal Impact:** If the sliding rock collides with the opposing mover, evaluate the space directly behind the opponent along `V`. If blocked by a wall or rock, it triggers an instant high-velocity CRUNCH win. If unblocked, the opponent is shoved backward 1 hex and the rock comes to a rest in their prior hex.

### MODIFIER 4: "Body Swap" (Quantum Translocation)
- **Concept:** Replace the standard 1-hex retreat bump with a coordinate exchange.
- **Trigger:** Mover A steps directly into the adjacent hex occupied by Mover B (without pushing a rock).
- **Mechanics:**
  1. Normal push check (space behind Mover B must be empty) is completely bypassed.
  2. Mover A and Mover B instantly swap hex coordinates: `Temp = Pos(A); Pos(A) = Pos(B); Pos(B) = Temp`.
  3. **Tactical Exploit:** A player can deliberately walk into a lethal corner trap, swap places with the opponent, and strand the opponent inside the hazard zone.
  4. **Sound / FX:** Emit a phase-shift synth sound and a spatial distortion wave between the two hex coordinates.

### MODIFIER 5: "Cursed Relic" (Hot Potato / Proximity Stun)
- **Concept:** A haunted rock radiates disruptive energy, forcing continuous interaction.
- **Trigger:** Game initialization randomly designates one rock as `CursedRockID` (highlighted with an erratic emerald flame/plasma pulse).
- **Mechanics:**
  1. If a mover ends their turn adjacent (distance = 1 hex) to the Cursed Rock, they absorb radiation and gain `Stunned = true`.
  2. On their subsequent turn, a stunned player cannot move; their turn action is forfeited (`Stunned` clears to false after the skip).
  3. If a player pushes the Cursed Rock, they are immune to its stun for that turn, and the rock slides/moves per standard rules.
  4. If the Cursed Rock is petrified or falls into a void hole, a new random active rock immediately inherits the curse.
